Seminars on Physics of Information 2026-27

Mestrado em Engenharia Física (Física da Informação)

Dep. Informática, Universidade do Minho

Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the course students should be able

  • To master several current issues in the foundations of Quantum Computing and its engineering.

Syllabus

The course consists of a weekly seminar on different topics in Physics of Information, covering quantum computing fountadions, methods, applications and engineering. Seminars will be delivered by several experts and academics in the area.

Some topics to be addressed:
Stabilizer formalism; Measurement-based quantum computation; Quantum open systems and quantum channels; von Neumann quantum logic; Quantum dynamic logics; Paraconsistent reasoning; Contextuality; Quantum programming languages, etc.
